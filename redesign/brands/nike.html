My Crazy Dream journals the rare path taken by Ironman triathlete Sarah Reinertsen, a woman possessed by a deep drive to push her body and her will to its absolute limits. We joined Sara on the Big Island of Hawaii a few days before the Ironman to capture her years-long journey and a tiny sliver of her optimism and determination.
