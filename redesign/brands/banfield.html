Banfield

Be Here

ClientBanfield Pet Hospital
AgencyConscious Minds
FormatBroadcast campaign
Year2023
Films3

Be Here is a multi-year brand platform for Banfield Pet Hospital, built on a simple truth: the relationship between a pet parent and their animal is unlike any other, and it deserves to be protected. Across the campaign, Banfield shows up not as a clinical necessity but as a quiet, constant partner, so pet parents can focus on being present for the moments that matter.
