FSC

Anthem

ClientForest Stewardship Council
AgencyZeus Jones
FormatBrand anthem film
Films1

You’ve probably seen the FSC logo a thousand times (literally), on tissue boxes, paper bags, greeting cards, furniture and just about every other paper or wood product you can imagine. But you likely haven’t paid too much attention to that little tree graphic or known what FSC really stands for. With this FSC Anthem Film , we aimed to change that by charting the many different aspects of responsible forestry and product creation that have made Forest Stewardship Council the leader in sustainable forestry.
