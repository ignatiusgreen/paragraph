More to the Story

ClientThe Chosen
AgencyConscious Minds
FormatContent series
StarringAmber Shana Williams
Episodes3

More to the Story is a content series for The Chosen, starring Amber Shana Williams. Each episode is companion content to the TV series, stepping outside the drama to unpack the history, culture and mythology behind the biblical narratives the show depicts: which laws Jesus broke, why he turned over the tables at the Temple, why Judas betrayed him. The result gives the show's audience more to bring to every episode.
