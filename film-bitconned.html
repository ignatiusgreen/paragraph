FEATURE DOCUMENTARY
TRUE CRIME

At only 24-years-old, the founders of Centra Tech were on course to become the next Mark Zuckerbergs. Through cutting-edge technology, Ray, Sam and Robert had solved the greatest problem of cryptocurrency by creating a usable crypto debit card. Having raised $32-million dollars, landing partnerships with Visa and Mastercard, and even garnering celebrity endorsements, Centra Tech was poised to take on the world. The only issue? The technology didn’t exist. Their dilemma: take the money and run from the SEC? Or double-down and try to turn Centra Tech into a real company?

WATCH ON NETFLIX

Anyone seriously considering investing in cryptocurrency should watch it, because the scams aren’t gone — they’ve only gotten more sophisticated.

An enraging account of opportunistic charlatanry, it’s also an entertaining ride in the so-called wild west of crypto.
